Spillover: Pour Puzzle

Privacy Policy · Solo Dev Studio · Effective date: 10 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game Spillover: Pour Puzzle ("the game") uses, why, and the choices you have. The game is made by Solo Dev Studio, an independent developer ("we").

1. Information stored on your device

Your progress (levels completed, coins, boosters, settings and purchases you made) is saved on your device. We do not receive this file. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

2. Information collected by services we use

The game does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. It uses the following third-party services, which collect some information automatically under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob shows ads. It may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (and an approximate location derived from it), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This is used to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later in the game's settings. How Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase helps us understand how the game is played (for example which levels are too hard) and find crashes. It collects an app instance ID, device and app information, and events in the game such as levels started and completed. Firebase privacy information.
  • Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore are used only if you enter a redeem code. The game then signs in anonymously: Firebase creates a random user ID, with no name, email or other account. We store that ID, the code you entered, the time, and your number in the order of players who redeemed it (for example "Founder #47"), so that each player can use a code only once. Firebase privacy information.
  • Google Play Games Services, if the game offers sign-in and you choose to sign in, links your Play Games profile to the game to save your progress in the cloud and for leaderboards. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing (through Unity In-App Purchasing) processes in-app purchases. We never see your payment details; we only receive a confirmation of what was bought, so the game can give it to you and restore it on a new device.

We do not sell your personal information. Information is sent over encrypted connections.

3. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

4. Your choices

  • Personalized ads: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on the device). Players in the EEA, UK and Switzerland can also change their consent in the game.
  • Remove ads: a one-time purchase in the game (No Ads or Premium) removes banner ads and ads between levels. Ads you choose to watch for a reward stay available.
  • Deletion: uninstalling the game deletes local data. Cloud saves can be deleted from your Google Play Games settings. If you redeemed a code, we can delete the record connected to your anonymous ID on request. You can also email us to ask about or delete information connected to your use of the game.

5. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
